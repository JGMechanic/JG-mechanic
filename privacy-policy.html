Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

JG Mobile Mechanic respects your privacy. This policy explains how we handle personal information when you use our website at jgmechanic.co.uk and our JG Mobile Mechanic customer and mechanic app.

1. Who we are

JG Mobile Mechanic is a mobile vehicle servicing and repair business in the United Kingdom. For privacy questions, contact jg.mechanic.73@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

Depending on how you use our services, we may process your name, email address, phone number, account details, vehicle registration, make, model, year, mileage, MOT and service information, repair requests, job history, photographs of vehicle work, invoices, and messages relating to your bookings.

Our app may also use device notification tokens to deliver service and job updates. Technical and security information may be processed to operate and protect the service.

3. How we use information

We use this information to manage customer accounts and vehicles, respond to service requests, carry out repairs, maintain job records, provide invoices, send relevant updates and reminders, and protect the security and reliability of our systems.

4. Legal bases

Where UK GDPR applies, our processing may be based on providing requested services or taking steps before a contract, complying with legal obligations, our legitimate interests in operating and securing the business, or consent where required.

5. Service providers

We use technology providers to operate the app, including Supabase for account and application data, Firebase for push notifications, and email delivery services for account and service messages. These providers may process information on our behalf in accordance with their applicable terms and safeguards.

6. Sharing information

We do not sell customer personal information. We may share information with service providers necessary to run the business, or where legally required.

7. Data retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably needed to provide services, maintain appropriate business and accounting records, resolve disputes, and meet legal obligations. Different types of records may have different retention periods.

8. Security

We use access controls and technical measures intended to protect information. No online system can be guaranteed completely secure.

9. Your rights and account deletion

You may contact us to request access, correction or deletion of your personal information, or to exercise other applicable data protection rights. Customers cannot currently delete accounts directly in the app.

To request account deletion, email jg.mechanic.73@gmail.com with the subject "Account deletion request". We may need to verify your identity. We will delete or anonymise eligible account data, while retaining records where required by law or legitimate business obligations.

10. International processing

Some technology providers may process data outside the UK. Where applicable, appropriate data protection safeguards are required.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our services or legal requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page.
